# Default writing rules

- Plain language; sentences under 25 words; paragraphs of 1–3 sentences
- Address the reader as "you"
- Explain any technical term the first time it appears
- One clear call to action per section; no more than 2 different CTAs per page
- Match spelling to the site's existing copy if project.md does not specify it
- No exclamation marks in headlines; no all-caps words
- Never invent facts, figures, prices, dates or quotes; leave a [TODO] instead
